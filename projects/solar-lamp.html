Smart Solar LED Bottle Lamp

A solar-powered LED lamp built into a bottle-style enclosure, made for the Petronas Solar Workshop at SMK Tun Abdul Razak. I led the R&D, designed the enclosure in Fusion 360, produced it on a 3D printer and taught students to build their own.

Design process

I designed and iterated 5 prototype enclosures in Autodesk Fusion 360, refining fit, strength and finish with each print until the final version was polished enough for students to assemble.

Production

I ran small-scale manufacturing on a 3D printer using PLA and PETG filament, producing 34 complete solar enclosures. The same setup also produced 1,000 custom education coins for school-wide use (see the Education Coins project).

Teaching & competition

I designed a STEM curriculum combining electronics, 3D design and rapid prototyping. My students, aged 14, learned to wire solar panels and breadboards, draw orthographic views and model their own enclosures in Tinkercad, a beginner-friendly 3D tool I chose for teaching.

I then selected and mentored a top team of 4 students and their teacher, preparing their prototype and presentation for the SAMNOVA national competition.
