STARS reward coins

The STARS coin is a school reward token: “STARS COIN” and a value on one side, the school crest on the other. I designed it in Fusion 360 and batch-printed 1,000 coins for school-wide use.

SAMNOVA 2026 keychains

For SAMNOVA 2026, I designed a set of keychains, including versions for PIBDP (Persatuan Inventif Bagan Datuk Perak) and the national-level (Peringkat Kebangsaan) round, each with a keyring hole built into the design.

Designing for two colours

Each design keeps the artwork on its own layers, so the text and logos print in a second colour against the base. Fine lettering and line art had to stay thick enough to print cleanly at coin size, from 28 mm to 59 mm across.
